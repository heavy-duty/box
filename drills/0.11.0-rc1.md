## Why a round is needed

This is the tenancy window (#279–#285, #287) and all of epic #253 (#288–#292).
It changes the seed (no default user, no tmux, no toolchain, no swap), the entry
model (root by default, `--user` opt-in), shared tenants and service grants,
firewall drop-ins, and the ACL shape (#298). An existing host needs
`box setup-host` to converge the ACL.
